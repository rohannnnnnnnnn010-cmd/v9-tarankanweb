Our Story

Built around reliability, transparency and long-term relationships.

Tarankan Exim LLP is an emerging Indian export company committed to supplying premium-quality products to international buyers. We work closely with verified manufacturers across India to provide products that meet global quality standards.

Our mission is to become a trusted global sourcing partner by offering reliable products, transparent business practices, competitive pricing and on-time deliveries.

Vision

01

To build a globally respected Indian trading house that transforms reliable manufacturing capabilities into lasting international partnerships, connecting the right products, suppliers, and markets with transparency, precision, and a distinctly Indian commitment to quality across borders and industries with confidence.

Mission

02

Our mission is to simplify global sourcing by connecting international buyers with dependable Indian manufacturers and products. We focus on understanding every requirement, coordinating the right supply solution, maintaining clear communication, and supporting quality, documentation, packaging, and delivery—creating a seamless trade experience built for repeat business and long-term partnerships worldwide.

Values

03

We believe every shipment represents a transaction. Integrity guides our decisions, quality shapes our standards, responsiveness strengthens relationships, innovation improves solutions, and customer focus keeps us accountable. We value transparency, consistency, respect, and the ambition to create value for partners.
