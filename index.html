About Tarankan

A reliable bridge between Indian supply and global demand.

Tarankan Exim LLP is a registered Limited Liability Partnership based in Maharashtra, India. We specialize in sourcing and exporting quality products from India to international markets. Our focus is on building long-term relationships through reliability, transparency and customer satisfaction.

What We Supply

Built for buyers who value consistency.

At Tarankan Exim LLP, we offer a growing range of machinery, automobile components, and mechanical parts sourced from reliable Indian manufacturers and suppliers. Our product portfolio is designed to meet the requirements of international buyers seeking dependable quality, competitive solutions, and consistent supply. We can support sourcing for industrial machinery, engineering components, automobile spare parts, mechanical assemblies, precision components, fasteners, tools, and related products based on buyer specifications. Our sourcing approach focuses on understanding technical requirements, material, dimensions, quantity, application, packaging, and delivery destination before identifying suitable supply options. Whether you require standard products or customized components, our team can coordinate with Indian suppliers to explore solutions aligned with your requirements. From initial enquiry and supplier coordination to quality requirements, packaging, documentation, and export logistics, Tarankan Exim LLP aims to provide a smooth and professional sourcing experience for international customers.
