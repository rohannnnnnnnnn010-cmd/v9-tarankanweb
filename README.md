# Tarankan Exim LLP Website

Static HTML/CSS/JS website for Tarankan Exim LLP.

## Pages
- index.html — Home
- about.html — About Us
- products.html — Products
- services.html — Export Services
- markets.html — Export Markets
- quality.html — Quality Assurance
- contact.html — Contact Us
- inquiry.html — Inquiry Form
- privacy.html — Privacy Policy
- terms.html — Terms & Conditions

## Notes
- Large visual areas use animated solid-color gradients/placeholders so product photography can be added later.
- The design uses a luxury editorial / premium-brand aesthetic rather than copying any third-party site directly.
- Replace the inquiry form action with your preferred backend/Formspree/Google Apps Script/Wix endpoint when deploying.
